## What Is Like a Dragon: Infinite Wealth?

Like a Dragon: Infinite Wealth is a role-playing game developed by Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio and published by SEGA. The story follows Ichiban Kasuga and Kazuma Kiryu through an adventure that connects familiar Japanese locations with Hawaii.

The game combines a character-driven narrative, turn-based battles, party development, exploration, and a broad selection of side activities. Players recruit and manage party members, choose combat roles, use character abilities, and adapt their tactics to different opponents.

Beyond the main story, the game offers optional challenges, local activities, and additional stories that expand the setting and introduce memorable characters.
